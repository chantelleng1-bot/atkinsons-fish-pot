Our story

A little seafood spot with a big welcome.

Atkinson's Fish Pot is run by the Atkinson family — and when you stop by, it's Mr. Atkinson, his wife and their son who'll greet you. Locals know it as a go-to for fresh fish, and visitors heading through White River keep finding their way here.

Fresh, every day

Snapper, lobster, shrimp and the day's catch — cooked fresh so it tastes like the sea it came from.

Family-run

Real Jamaican hospitality from a family who cooks like they're feeding their own.

Eat in or take away

Seating is cozy, so takeaway is a favourite. Call ahead, or order delivery on 7Krave.
